## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Blood of Dawnwalker**. This trainer provides optional quality-of-life and progression controls for Coen's human and vampire gameplay loops. It can protect health and stamina during combat, reduce ability downtime, preserve resources, and pause the campaign clock while exploring Vale Sangora.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more relaxed single-player experience, faster experimentation with Coen's human and vampire builds, easier combat practice, or more time to explore Vale Sangora and complete optional content.

> **Important:** Use third-party tools only at your own risk. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Unlimited Health** — Prevents Coen from losing health during exploration and combat.
- **Freeze Campaign Time** — Pauses the passage of in-game time while you travel, investigate locations, and prepare for quests.
- **Easy Skill Progression** — Supports faster development of human, vampire, and shared skill paths.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Stamina** — Keeps stamina available for attacks, blocks, dodges, and movement.

### 🧰 Inventory

- **Inventory Protection** — Helps preserve consumables and other usable resources during extended journeys.

### 🌍 World

- **Enhanced Exploration** — Makes it easier to investigate mysteries, use Focus, and explore day and night routes without interruption.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after loading into the world. Movement remains governed by the game's normal controls, while protected stamina and paused time make long journeys and exploration easier. Nighttime vampire abilities provide additional traversal options, so activate movement-related settings before entering areas that require supernatural mobility.

### Tools & Inventory

Open the inventory normally and activate resource protection before using healing items or other consumables. The trainer is intended to reduce unnecessary resource management while preserving the game's equipment and weight-based inventory structure.

### World & Environment

The campaign is built around a limited number of days and nights, with choices, travel, investigations, and quests advancing the clock. Use the time option when examining locations, following leads with Focus, or planning which objectives to pursue. Disable it before story transitions if you want the campaign to progress normally.

### Combat & Weapons

Activate health and stamina protection before entering a fight. Coen uses human swordplay and abilities during the day, while nighttime gameplay grants vampire powers and different combat approaches. Unlimited ability use is best used for testing skill combinations, practicing directional attacks, and exploring both combat styles.
